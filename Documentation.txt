Samuel Pennington:
	Player:
		Designed basic player movement and interaction with puzzles
	Camera:
		Created a camera follower that tracks the player, but has custom 
		boundries and a radius for controlled movement
	Door:
		Teleports the player to another room, can be kept hidden until a condition is met
	Math System:
		A math system that contains multiple puzzle objects and their values that are 
		then calculated and compared to a list of possible answers
	Tile Puzzle:
		Created Tile plates and pick-ups allowing puzzles with carefully placing 
		objects in the right order
	Growing Equation Puzzle:
		Designed a unique puzzle involving a procedurally growing equation using similar 
		features to the math system
	Enemy X:
		Created a design for a base enemy with models and animations
	Layout:
		Designed a basic layout that presents all current aspects of the overworld
	Models:
		Designed models for Enemy X, Most puzzle objects, and the wall tiles.
